🧹 Mass Friends Remover for Facebook — Bulk Unfriender

Tired of scrolling through hundreds of old friends one by one? Clean up your Facebook friends list in minutes. Select the people you want to remove and unfriend them all in one click — right on your own Facebook Friends page.

✨ What you can do
✅ Bulk unfriend — select many friends and remove them together
🎯 Pick exactly who to remove with a checkbox on each friend
🔀 Sort your list by name, mutual friends, or profile
⚡ One-click "Select all" and "Deselect all"
🕒 Smart, randomized delay between removals to stay safe
🛑 Stop anytime — you're always in control

🔒 100% private & local
Everything runs inside your browser using your own logged-in session. Your friend list never leaves your computer. No account, no sign-up, no server, no tracking.

⚙️ How it works
1️⃣ Click the toolbar icon to open your Friends page
2️⃣ Press "Load all" to bring in every friend
3️⃣ Tick the friends you want to remove
4️⃣ Click "Unfriend selected" and confirm

Perfect for spring-cleaning your account, removing inactive friends, or starting fresh. 🌱

⚠️ Note: Facebook may limit very fast changes, so a safe delay is used between removals.
